Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside JobSnap. There's no account. Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Restore your iPhone backup, or in JobSnap go to Settings › Export & Import › Export All Data, save the .zip to Files or iCloud Drive, then on the new iPhone choose "Restore from a Backup" during setup (or Settings › Export & Import › Import).

Will deleting a photo from my Photos library remove it from a job?

No. JobSnap keeps its own copy of every job photo.

Are my internal notes shown to customers?

Never by default. New notes are internal. Only notes you mark "Show on Customer Report" appear in reports.

How do I send a report?

Open a job › Report › Create Report, check the preview, then tap Share to send it by Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or save it to Files.

How do I cancel JobSnap Pro?

Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › JobSnap. To restore a purchase, open JobSnap › Settings › JobSnap Pro › Restore Purchases.

Can I use JobSnap for my trade?

Yes — electricians, HVAC, plumbers, cleaners, painters, landscapers, detailers and more. Templates are fully customizable.
