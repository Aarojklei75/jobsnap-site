Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

JobSnap is built to keep your work on your iPhone. This policy explains what JobSnap does — and doesn't do — with your information.

The short version: JobSnap does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. There is no JobSnap account and no JobSnap server. Your customers, jobs, photos, notes and reports stay on your device.

Information you enter

Business details, customer contact information, job records, photos, checklists, notes and materials you add are saved locally in JobSnap on your device. The developer cannot see or access this information.

Camera and photos

JobSnap asks for camera access only when you take a job photo. Photos you choose from your library are copied into JobSnap so they stay with the job. JobSnap never uploads your photos.

Sharing and exports

Reports and backups leave your device only when you share or export them using the iOS share sheet. Once shared, they are handled by the app or person you send them to.

Backups

Your JobSnap data is included in your device's iCloud or computer backup, governed by Apple's privacy policy. Exported .zip backups are files you control.

Purchases

JobSnap Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Subscription status is checked on-device using Apple's StoreKit.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

JobSnap contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

JobSnap is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any customer, job or photo in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your device (except copies in your own backups or exports).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
